Privacy Policy — Ledger

Last updated: [DATE]

Ledger is a personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What data is collected

None. Ledger does not have a server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not transmit any of your expense data anywhere. Everything you enter — dates, amounts, categories, notes — is saved only in your device's local browser storage.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored locally using your browser's localStorage, scoped to this app. It stays on your device and is never uploaded, synced, or shared. If you use the app on more than one device, they will not automatically share data — use the built-in Backup/Restore feature to move data between devices yourself.

Third-party services

The app loads two external resources purely for display purposes: Google Fonts (for typography) and a charting library (Chart.js) from a public CDN. These are static files and do not receive any of your expense data. Standard web requests for these files may include normal technical information such as your IP address and browser type, handled according to the respective providers' own policies (Google Fonts, cdnjs/Cloudflare).

Data deletion

You can erase all locally stored data at any time from the app's Data & Backup tab ("Erase all data"), or by clearing this site's data from your browser settings.

Children's privacy

Ledger does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated accordingly.
